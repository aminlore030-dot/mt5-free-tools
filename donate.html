Support

Donations are optional, always

Every Expert Advisor and indicator here is free to download, today and in future versions. Donations only exist for people who want to help fund development.

Send a voluntary donation

A donation never unlocks a file, a feature, a version or support priority. If you donate, you get exactly what everyone else gets: the same free tools.

No donation link is configured at the moment, so there is nothing to pay and nothing to click here. Reporting bugs, suggesting improvements or starring the repository helps just as much.

Send a donation Get the tools instead

    What donations pay for

    • Development time for new tools and fixes.
    • Testing on real market data and demo accounts.
    • Writing and updating documentation.

    What is never collected

    • No card details, ever. This site has no payment processing.
    • No accounts, passwords or personal data.
    • No trading account numbers or broker credentials.

    If a donation link is configured, it points to an external service that handles everything on its own pages.
