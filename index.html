Getting started

Installing takes about a minute

  1. Download the .ex5 file from the tool page.
  2. In MetaTrader 5 open File, then Open Data Folder.
  3. Drop Expert Advisors in MQL5/Experts and indicators in MQL5/Indicators.
  4. Restart MT5 or refresh the Navigator panel, then drag the tool onto a chart.

What this site is not

No accounts, no upsells

There is no login, no trial, no locked "pro" version and no payment flow. The download button is the whole product. If a tool needs configuration, the settings are documented on its page.

Automated trading carries risk. Nothing here is financial advice, and past behaviour of a strategy does not predict future results.
